Aji de gallina

Description

This is a delicious Peruvian dish, made with shredded chicken in a creamy sauce, rice, eggs and olives

  • Ingredients:
    1. Chicken
    2. Aji amarillo (yellow chili pepper)
    3. Milk
    4. Bread
    5. Onion
    6. Garlic
    7. Cheese
    8. Potatoes
  • Instructions:
    • Cook the chicken and shred it.
    • Sauté onions and garlic, then add aji amarillo paste.
    • Add milk and bread to create a creamy sauce.
    • Mix in the shredded chicken and cook until heated through.
    • Serve over boiled potatoes and garnish with cheese.
