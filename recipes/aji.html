Aji

Description

This is a traditional Peruvian dish made with spicy sauce and various ingredients.

Ingredients

  • Aji amarillo (Peruvian chili)
  • Coconut milk
  • Onions
  • Garlic
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Cook the chicken and shred it.
  2. Sauté onions and garlic, then add the aji amarillo paste.
  3. Add milk and bread to make a creamy sauce, then mix in the shredded chicken.
  4. Serve over boiled potatoes and garnish with cheese.
