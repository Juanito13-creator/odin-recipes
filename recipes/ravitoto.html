Ravitoto

Description

This is a traditional Malagasy dish made with rice, meat, and various vegetables.

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Meat (beef or lamb)
  • Vegetables (onions, tomatoes, carrots)
  • Garlic
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Cook the rice according to package instructions.
  2. Season the meat with salt and pepper, then cook until done.
  3. Prepare the vegetables by chopping them into small pieces.
  4. Combine the cooked rice, meat, and vegetables in a large bowl and mix well.
