Ravitoto

Description

This a traditional malagasy dish made with pork and crushed cassava leaves.

  • Ingredients:
    1. Pork
    2. Ravitoto (crushed cassava leaves)
    3. Coconut milk
    4. Garlic
    5. Onion
    6. Ginger
    7. Salt and pepper
  • Instructions:
    • Marinate the pork with garlic, ginger, salt, and pepper.
    • Sauté onions and garlic, then add the marinated pork.
    • Add ravitoto and coconut milk, and simmer until the pork is tender.
    • Serve hot with rice.
