Ravitoto

Description

This is a traditional Malagasy dish made with pork and crushed cassava leaves.

Ingredients

  • Pork
  • Ravitoto (crushed cassava leaves)
  • Coconut milk
  • Garlic
  • Onion
  • Ginger
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Marinate the pork with garlic, ginger, salt, and pepper.
  2. Sauté onions and garlic, then add the marinated pork.
  3. Add ravitoto and coconut milk, and simmer until the pork is tender.
  4. Serve hot with rice.
