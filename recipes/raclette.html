Raclette

Description

This is a traditional Swiss dish made with melted cheese and various accompaniments.

  • Ingredients:
    1. Raclette cheese
    2. French bread
    3. Onions
    4. Garlic
    5. Onion
    6. Ginger
    7. Salt and pepper
  • Instructions:
    • Heat the raclette cheese in a pan until melted.
    • Toast the French bread and serve with the melted cheese.
    • Prepare the onions and garlic as accompaniments.
    • Serve hot with the prepared accompaniments.
