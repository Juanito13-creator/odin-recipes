Raclette

Description

This is a traditional Swiss dish made with melted cheese and various accompaniments.

Ingredients

  • Raclette cheese
  • French bread
  • Onions
  • Garlic
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Heat the raclette cheese in a pan until melted.
  2. Toast the French bread and serve with the melted cheese.
  3. Prepare the onions and garlic as accompaniments.
  4. Serve hot with the prepared accompaniments.
